THE SHORT VERSION

Keep your friends
guessing.

  1. Make it believable.

    The host prepares 10 questions, each with a real answer and a convincing lie.

  2. Bluff for 30 seconds.

    Players submit an answer. Know the truth? You can still write it. Miss the timer? Your option sits this round out.

  3. Find the truth.

    You have 30 seconds to vote for one option. You can’t vote for your own submission. Duplicate answers stay separate — choose carefully.

  4. See who fooled whom.

    The host reveals players in any order. Points are awarded on each reveal: correct answer +2; host lie −1; another player’s bluff +1 to its author. A duplicate of the truth still counts as a player’s bluff. No vote earns 0.

The host starts each question after revealing every player. After all 10, the most points wins. Ties share the win.
